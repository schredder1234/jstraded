Before you ask

Is this record real?

Every pick is posted publicly on @jstraded before tip-off, then graded from the box score after the game. Nothing is deleted or reworded — the losses on this page are the proof, and the X timestamps are there for anyone to audit.

Which sportsbook are the lines from?

Each pick names the book whose line it was bet at — it's on every card and every row above. Lines move, so the one you get may differ; the record always grades the posted line, never a better one after the fact.

How much does this cost?

Nothing. The picks post free and in public because the record is the product — it has to earn trust before it earns anything else. If that ever changes, this free graded record stays up.

What's a unit?

Your standard bet size — every ticket here risks exactly one. The slider above converts the whole record into dollars at whatever your unit is.

Is this betting advice?

No. It's the tracked record of a model, shown win and lose. If you bet, bet only what you can afford to lose, and only if you're of legal age where you live.

How to read this page
Ticket
A pick that passed every pre-game check and was posted publicly before tip-off. Tickets are the record that counts.
Watch
A pick the model liked but that failed one sanity check (matchup, minutes, form, or sharp-book agreement). Tracked for honesty, never posted as a play.
Void
The player was scratched or didn't play, so the bet graded nothing — same as a sportsbook voiding the prop. Voids never count in the record.
Unit
One standard bet size. Results are shown in units so the record reads the same at any bankroll.
Closing line value (CLV)
How much better our price was than the market's final price. Beating the close consistently is the best early sign a betting process is real; luck shows up in results, skill shows up in CLV.
Checks
Four pre-game questions asked of every pick: does the matchup agree, are the minutes safe, does recent form agree, do sharp books agree. A pick needs at least 3 of 4 to post.

No pick is removed or reworded after posting. Losses stay on the board. None of this is betting advice.
